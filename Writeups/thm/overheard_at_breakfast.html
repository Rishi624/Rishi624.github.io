// Challenge

The breakfast terrace is loud this morning, clinking cutlery, espresso machines, the usual chatter. One guest couldn't help but linger at a nearby table, seeing more of a conversation than they were meant to. When the table's occupant stepped away for a refill, they seized the moment and grabbed a screenshot before it could disappear. Somewhere in that conversation is enough to track down an account nobody was supposed to find.

After downloading the screenshot, I carefully read through the entire conversation instead of immediately looking for hidden metadata. Two pieces of information immediately stood out:

  • An email address shared in the Discord conversation.
  • A sentence mentioning a free tool that starts with the letter "G" which allows users to upload a profile and link all of their social media accounts.

At first, I searched the email address directly on Google, both with and without quotation marks, but no useful results were returned. Since the email wasn't indexed anywhere, I shifted my attention to the second clue.

// Following the "G" Clue

The conversation specifically described the service as:

Free tool that lets you upload your profile and link other media accounts.

Using that description, I searched Google with the following query:

free tool start with g which you can link all your accounts and upload profile pic

Among the results, Gravatar matched the description perfectly. Gravatar allows users to create a public profile, upload an avatar, and link various social media accounts.

// Finding the Hidden Profile

After identifying Gravatar, I searched for a Gravatar email lookup tool. Using the email address extracted from the Discord screenshot, I was able to locate the user's public Gravatar profile.

Browsing to the profile revealed a Base64 encoded string:

VEhNe1MzY3JlVF9QcjBmaWwzX0g0c19iMzNuX0lkZW50MWZpM2R9

// Decoding the Message

The string clearly resembled Base64 encoding, so I opened CyberChef and used the From Base64 recipe to decode it.

The decoded output revealed the challenge flag.

// Flag

THM{S3creT_Pr0fil3_H4s_b33n_Ident1fi3d}
